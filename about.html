About NepalCreates

A creator management agency concept built around one idea: Nepali creators deserve professional support.

Our story

NepalCreates began as a question: why is it so hard for talented creators in Nepal to turn their work into steady, professional opportunities?

Creators were handling content, contracts, and outreach alone, while brands struggled to find the right people. We imagined an agency that handles the in-between work so both sides can focus on what they do best.

NepalCreates is a fictional agency created for a BIT Web Technology project. The story, services, and details on this site are a concept, not a real business.

Who we are

A small, creator-focused team concept that combines management, marketing, and content planning for digital creators and the businesses that partner with them.

Our approach to creator management

We start by listening. Then we set goals, choose partnerships carefully, agree on terms in writing, and review results together so every project teaches something.

Our mission

To help Nepal's creators build sustainable careers and help brands run honest, effective collaborations.

Our vision

A creative economy in Nepal where creators are respected as professionals and partnerships are fair for everyone involved.

Our core values

Four principles guide how we make decisions.

Creators come first

A creator's voice and wellbeing shape every plan we make.

Transparency

Clear terms, honest advice, and no hidden conditions.

Long-term growth

We favour steady progress over quick wins.

Respect

For creators, for audiences, and for Nepal's culture.
