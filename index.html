A bridge between creators and brands

Many Nepali creators have the audience and the ideas but not the time to negotiate deals, plan campaigns, or build a professional profile. Many businesses want to work with creators but do not know where to start.

NepalCreates sits in the middle. We learn what a creator wants to build, learn what a brand needs to achieve, and set up collaborations that suit both.

Why creators and brands choose to work with us

Our approach is simple: be clear, be fair, and keep the creator's voice at the centre.

  • Creator-firstPartnerships are chosen to fit a creator's style, not just a budget.
  • Transparent termsDeliverables, timelines, and payment terms are written down before work starts.
  • Personal supportEach creator and brand has a named contact, not a ticket number.
  • Local understandingWe work with Nepali audiences, languages, and platforms in mind.
